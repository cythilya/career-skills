# Resume Principles

Source: 「如何寫出一份出色的履歷」 https://www.cythilya.tw/2024/04/19/how-to-create-an-outstanding-resume/

(O) = good example, (X) = bad example. Examples are illustrative; never copy their
numbers into a user's resume.

## 0. Core idea

- The resume is a business card and a short self-introduction whose job is to earn the
  interview.
- Readers: boss, manager, or HR. They care whether the candidate can solve current
  problems, bring revenue, and raise team quality.
- **Show concrete results and achievements (具體的成果與成就). Do not just list duties or
  describe what you are learning.**
- A reviewer may spend only 15–30 minutes per resume, sometimes on short notice —
  key points must be visible at a glance.

## 1. Know yourself and the market (tailoring)

- Interviews are a two-way choice (雙向選擇).
- Match strengths (technology, expertise, experience, education, language) to the target
  job, then write the resume for that job.
- Tailoring examples:
  - Full-stack job → list both front-end and back-end skills.
  - Remote job → highlight remote experience, self-management, teamwork, communication.
  - Cross-border company → English resume; highlight cross-timezone and cross-cultural
    collaboration.

## 2. Structure and layout

Order, top to bottom:

1. **簡述 Summary**
2. **經歷 Experience** — most important; include numbers where possible
3. **學歷 Education** — with rich experience, only the highest degree
4. **技能 Skills**
5. **作品 Works**
6. **自傳 About / self-description** — optional; only when the user asks for it

- Use clean layout; **bold** (or underline) keywords so they stand out.
- Review the whole resume and reorder so the key points come first.

## 3. 簡述 Summary

- Pick 3–5 strengths (e.g. 前端開發、效能、SEO), drawn from project achievements or feedback
  from supervisors and colleagues.
- Write them as about **3 sentences** that show a clear specialty.
- (X) 「我熱愛學習、喜歡挑戰自己，擁有良好的溝通能力。」 — not a specialty, doesn't show value.
- (X) 「我是一位正在學習前端技能的轉職生／菜鳥工程師。」 — "learning / career-changer /
  rookie" signals no immediate capability. Stress professionalism instead.

## 4. 經歷 Experience

- **If there is data, write the data. If not, state the contribution** so the reader sees
  the value.
- (O) Built front-end performance monitoring with Cypress, Lighthouse, and Sentry;
  **bundle size −24%, FCP +25%, LCP +24%**. Numbers let the reader estimate the benefit.
- (O) Led the front-end team: created a training process, ran training sessions and a
  reading club, raising team skills and building a knowledge-sharing culture.
- (X) 「實作 XX、導入 OO、重構 ZZ。」 — a meaningless list. Add the effect, e.g. after
  introducing OO, bugs fell 50% versus the previous year.
- **Achievements, not projects:** organize experience by achievement, not by project.
  A resume that lists projects (「專案 A：負責…」「專案 B：使用…」) shows what the person
  worked on, not what they accomplished, and the reader has to dig for the value.
  - Put every achievement under the job (company) where it happened. A separate 「專案經歷」
    section tied to jobs is merged into those jobs.
  - Extract the achievements and numbers from each project, then regroup them by outcome
    (e.g. performance, quality and testing, revenue or conversion, team and process).
    Similar results from different projects become one stronger bullet.
  - Keep the project name only as context inside the bullet when it helps, e.g.
    「優化電商結帳頁（XX 專案）效能，LCP 降低 30%」. The bullet starts with the result or
    action, not the project name.
  - Drop project descriptions that carry no achievement (what the product is, team size,
    tech stack listings). Move the tech to 技能 if it supports the target role.
  - Side projects or open-source work not tied to a job go to 作品.
  - (X) 「**XX 會員系統**：使用 React、Redux 開發會員功能，負責登入、註冊頁面。」
  - (O) 「**重構會員系統登入流程**，導入表單驗證與錯誤追蹤，登入失敗客訴減少 40%。」
- **Ordering:** put technically difficult and leadership items first — they lead to deeper
  interview conversations.
- **Verbs:** start with active verbs — 導入、建立、提升、優化、解決. Avoid passive 協助、支援.
  - (X) 「協助重構 XX 系統。」
  - (O) 「導入模組化開發，藉由優化模組，提升產品品質、穩定性和開發工作流程效率。」
  - (O) 「導入視覺化測試，建立更全面的測試工作流程，增加測試覆蓋率，發現並解決潛在問題。」
- **Internship only:** same principle — school, interest, internship, problems solved,
  features built, collaboration and praise from supervisor/colleagues, improvements made.
  Once there is formal work experience, shorten or remove internships without special
  contribution or relevance.
- **Off-target experience** (e.g. TA, PM when applying as an engineer): trim it, or
  connect it to the target role (PM → coordination and prioritization; designer → UX and
  communicating with designers).
- **Career-change gap:** do not write 「自學」. Real experience such as freelance or
  self-employment is better.

## 5. 學歷 Education

- **No formal work experience (new graduate):** education is the focus. List school,
  major, thesis, scholarships, awards, internships, freelance/part-time work — written as
  achievements.
  - (O) Research topic, published paper, competitions, scholarships, awards, and how the
    research was applied.
  - (X) 「主修 NNN 科系，修習 OOO 與 PPP 課程。」 — doesn't show actual ability.
- **Already working:** only the highest degree, or education with special meaning (study
  abroad, scholarship, award).

## 6. 技能 Skills

- List the skills you **want to be hired for**, not every skill you have (targeting React →
  jQuery can be omitted).
- Group by category, e.g.:
  - Front-end: JavaScript, React.js, Styled Components, GraphQL, TypeScript
  - Testing: React Testing Library, Jest, WebdriverIO, visual testing (Chromatic, Percy)
  - Tools: Git, Webpack, Chrome DevTools, Lighthouse, Figma, Search Console, Storybook

## 7. 作品 Works

- Show skills, preferred technology, and expertise beyond work experience:
  - **Blog** — learning notes, technical sharing, project retrospectives.
  - **GitHub** — code, projects, open-source contributions.
  - **Social media / podcast** — quicker to produce, less in-depth.
- Use full URLs.

## 8. 自傳 About

- Optional. Include it only when the user asks; by default the resume ends at 作品.
- Add what the rest of the resume does not say (motivation, way of working, the team
  they want to join). Do not repeat results already in 簡述 or 經歷.
- Show **personality traits through work actions**, not a life chronology.
- (O) 「我致力於幫助團隊成長，因此建立培訓流程、提供培訓和組織讀書會，以提高團隊的技術技能。」
- (X) Family upbringing, being independent, liking challenges, not fearing failure — may be
  true but unrelated to work; express them through work examples instead.
- Be confident: compare with your past self, not others; stress strengths.
- Avoid 「我會更努力」 — it undermines confidence and professionalism.

## 9. Other details

- Remove: headshot (no help, may cause bias), place of residence, family situation,
  excessive school details, and anything else the interviewer doesn't need.
- Space between Chinese and English characters; consistent, correct capitalization of
  proper nouns (GitHub, not Github).
- Write full links instead of hiding them behind 「看更多」.
- Avoid 「自學」「初學」; don't emphasize career change or non-engineering background.
- Language ability: give certificates or scores. (O) 「多益 700 分」 (X) 「英文流利」.
- Bold keywords to signal 「跟我聊這些！」.

## Quick checklist

| ID | Check |
|---|---|
| P1 | Sections in order: 簡述 → 經歷 → 學歷 → 技能 → 作品 → 自傳 (自傳 only if requested) |
| P2 | Summary: ~3 sentences, 3–5 concrete specialties, no generic soft skills or "learning/rookie" wording |
| P3 | Every experience bullet shows a result (number) or a clear contribution |
| P4 | Bullets start with active verbs; no 協助／支援 |
| P5 | Hard/leadership items first within each job |
| P6 | Off-target experience trimmed or connected to the target role |
| P7 | Education length fits career stage |
| P8 | Skills limited to what the target job needs, grouped by category |
| P9 | Works listed with full URLs |
| P10 | About (if requested) shows traits through work actions, adds something new, no 「我會更努力」 |
| P11 | No photo, address, family details, or other unneeded personal info |
| P12 | No 自學／初學／轉職／菜鳥／學習中 framing |
| P13 | Language ability as certificate/score |
| P14 | Chinese–English spacing, proper-noun capitalization, full links |
| P15 | 3–5 bolded keywords per section |
| P16 | Tailored to the JD (if given) |
| P17 | Experience organized by achievement and numbers under each job, not by project |
